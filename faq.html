About the technology

Technical questions answered directly.

Is Q2 just compression?
No. Compression works by finding patterns and redundancy in data and encoding them more efficiently. Q2 operates in a different mathematical framework entirely â restructuring data at a geometric and mathematical level, rather than searching for statistical patterns. The practical difference: Q2 can work on data types â like encrypted files â that compression cannot reduce at all.
Does this break mathematical limits?
No. Shannon's entropy limit governs statistical compression. It applies to schemes that exploit pattern and redundancy in data. Whether it constrains a bijective structural transformation operating in a different mathematical domain is precisely the research question we are investigating. We are not claiming to break anything.
Is this quantum computing?
No. Q2 runs on standard computing hardware. The mathematical lineage of the approach draws from quantum-inspired frameworks, but the system requires no specialised quantum hardware at any current stage of the programme.
What data types does Q2 work best on?
Structured data with consistent internal form â database exports, schema-conformant logs, scientific time-series measurements, structured configuration files. These are the data categories showing the strongest results in current laboratory testing. Already-encrypted files and already-compressed media show minimal benefit. We document both.
How do you verify that data is restored correctly?
A cryptographic fingerprint is taken of the original file. After reconstruction, the same fingerprint is taken of the output. If they match exactly, the transformation is confirmed correct. This test is run on every individual file in our test suite â not batched or averaged. A single failure is a test failure, full stop.
About the programme

Where things stand, stated plainly.

What stage is the technology at?
Active laboratory evaluation. The Q2 system has produced verifiable results on structured data under controlled conditions, with cryptographic verification passing on all current test cases. Independent third-party replication has not yet been completed. That is the next major validation milestone, and it is currently in preparation.
Has anyone outside Implexus validated the results?
Not yet. All current results are from our own controlled laboratory testing. Controlled demonstrations have been conducted with government and institutional stakeholders. Independent third-party replication â where an external lab runs our documented tests on their own infrastructure without our involvement â is the next step.
How long has this been in development?
Foundational research began in the late 1980s. Applied systems development expanded from 2013. Controlled demonstrations with external stakeholders have been ongoing since 2022. We describe this as "multiple decades" because that is what is accurate.
Why does Implexus not publish results publicly yet?
Because current results are from our own laboratory. Publishing internal findings as validated external claims would be inaccurate. The standard we hold ourselves to is: independent replication first, then publication.
